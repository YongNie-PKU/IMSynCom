Repeated Random Simulation

In each run, the currently selected parameter is resampled from the specified distribution while all other parameters retain their table values. The CSV records the complete sampled parameters, stability status, and full time series for every run.

Select a random distribution to begin; the browser will prompt for a CSV save location.
Fitting basis:First fit the monoculture net growth rate r−m, then combine it with the independently measured mortality rate m to obtain r; calculate aᵢⱼ from stable endpoint absolute biomasses in pairwise cocultures. Intermediate measurement time tₘ: h
Experimental parameter definitions:
B₀: Actual biomass at the start of monoculture growth or simulation, in the same units as K.
B(tₘ): Actual biomass of the monoculture at intermediate time tₘ, used to fit the net growth rate.
tₘ: Culture time at which B(tₘ) is measured, in h.
m: Independently measured mortality rate, in h⁻¹, requiring m ≥ 0.
K: Actual biomass at the monoculture steady state, i.e., the monoculture environmental carrying capacity.
r: Growth rate fitted from the monoculture curve, in h⁻¹; the model net growth rate is r−m, requiring r > m.
Cᵢⱼ: Stable endpoint actual biomass of species i when cocultured with species j.
aᵢⱼ: Dimensionless effect of species j on species i; positive values indicate facilitation, negative values indicate inhibition, and diagonal elements satisfy aᵢᵢ = −1.
