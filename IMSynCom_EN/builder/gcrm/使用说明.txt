IMSynCom Blocks gCRM v1.4

1. Keep the relative directory structure of index.html, assets, and image unchanged.
2. Name the logo logo.png and place it in the image directory.
3. Double-click index.html to run it offline in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
4. The canvas supports species, resource, and metabolite nodes; metabolites are represented as triangles.
5. An ‘uptake/growth’ edge points from a resource or metabolite to a species and has parameters c and Y.
6. A ‘resource transformation’ edge points from a species to an output metabolite; the parameter panel on the right lists all input resources or metabolites connected to that species through uptake edges.
7. A separate transformation yield D_iβα can be specified for each input Q_α. The corresponding production flux is D_iβα X_i u_iα; total production of the same product is the sum of fluxes from all inputs, and metabolites cannot be produced independently of substrate uptake.
8. Bidirectional edges are automatically drawn on opposite sides of the connection and do not overlap.
9. After simulation, the page displays species dynamics, endpoint biomass, a relative-abundance pie chart, and endpoint resource/metabolite concentrations.
